# Production Engineer

A production engineer decides how work should be done on a vehicle line. The engineer does not run the shift. Supervisors do that. A shift is the block of hours one group is on duty. When the line finishes too little, quality gets worse, or a machine fails, the engineer changes that method.

## Mandate

The engineer decides how each station should do the work. A station is a single work position on the line. Supervisors follow that written method while they run the shift.

## How the work runs

The day starts with the results, a walk of the line, and a meeting with the last shift. The engineer then looks for why those results are off. The problem can come from how people make the product, how the station is arranged, or the machine. The engineer studies all of those causes as one investigation. They have to tell the causes apart before choosing a fix. They also set how often machines should be checked and repaired.

The supervisor tells the engineer what is happening on the line now. They work with quality on defects, maintenance on repairs, and planning on whether the line can meet the schedule.

A change is not finished when the engineer has an idea. They check that the change is safe for people to run. They check that people can still finish the required work in the allowed time. They write the instruction the people at the station will follow. The change must be approved before the line uses it. If that step is skipped, a bad change becomes the way everyone works. They update the instruction so the next shift has the current method.

## Decisions

Production numbers jump around, so the engineer must decide if a drop is real. They choose which problem is worth setting other work aside for. They may recommend a stop, or a small adjustment to a machine setting. The supervisor decides whether the line actually comes to a stop.

## Boundaries

Supervisors assign people to stations while the line is running. Maintenance repairs the machine after the fault is already known. Planning sets the schedule the line is asked to meet. The engineer tells planning how much the line can still produce. With quality, the engineer decides if a product waits for a check or moves on.
