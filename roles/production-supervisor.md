# Production Supervisor

A production supervisor leads one section of a moving vehicle line for a shift. A shift is the block of hours one group is on duty. The product moves from station to station while that shift is working. The supervisor is responsible for the people in that area and for their safety. The area must finish the planned work at the required quality.

## Mandate

The supervisor decides who stands at each station for the shift. A station is a single work position on the line. They check that the person is trained for that station. They place a trained person there when someone is absent. An untrained person at a station can cause a safety problem or a quality problem.

## How the work runs

Each new shift starts with the supervisor on the floor. They check the people, the stations, and the issues left by the last shift. New problems arrive while the supervisor is walking the area. Several of those problems often look equally urgent at once. Maintenance, planning, quality, and engineering call the supervisor when a plan has to change on the floor. The call may be a stop, a missing part, a quality problem, or a machine that has stopped. Urgent contact with those groups goes by phone or radio. The contact is finished only when the other person confirms they are acting. At the end of the shift, the supervisor tells the next supervisor what happened. The report includes corrections that are not written down yet.

## Decisions

When something goes wrong, the supervisor chooses which problem to handle first. The call cannot wait, because the line is still moving. A delay here can slow the areas before this section and after it. The supervisor then keeps the area running, slows it, sends the product along a different path, or stops it. A safety risk stops the work for that whole area. A quality problem that might still spread also stops the work. A defect already set aside can be fixed while the rest of the area keeps running.

## Boundaries

The supervisor follows the instructions that are already in use. Other people design the method, the machines, and the schedule past this shift. After maintenance returns a machine, the supervisor brings the area back onto the schedule.
